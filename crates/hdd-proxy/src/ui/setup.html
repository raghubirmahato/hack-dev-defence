HDDHack Dev Defence

Set up your control panel in a minute.

Create the owner account, then add your website. HDD tells you honestly when a site is not protected yet.

  • Owner account stays on this machine (loopback only)
  • Passwords are hashed; sessions expire; failed logins are rate limited
  • The wizard generates the DNS, Caddy, Cloudflare, nginx or Vercel steps

Step 1 of 2 · owner account

First-run setup

Create the Owner account. This page is only offered while no account exists and only from the machine running HDD (loopback): a remote browser is refused. After this step the setup page returns 404.

  • At least 12 characters
  • At least three of: upper case, lower case, digits, symbols
  • Does not contain the username

Step 2 of 2 · put HDD in front of a site

Owner account created

You are signed in. Add your domain and origin next: the wizard generates the DNS/Caddy/Cloudflare/nginx or Vercel steps, runs the origin lockdown check and verifies protection.

Remember: a site is not protected until the origin is locked down and verification passes.
