# J-07 Owner completes the onboarding checklist

Personas: [Maya](../personas/maya.md), [Alex](../personas/alex.md). Emotional state is a design aid, not a measured value.

Trigger: the account exists and the org overview shows the onboarding checklist for the first time.

## Steps

| Step | User action | System response | Screen | Data collected | Error path | Emotional state | Copy key | Metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Fills in the profile fields. | The wizard keeps the values and shows the profile step. | S-035 | name, timezone | ERR-VAL-001: The form lists the invalid profile field and keeps the entered values. | calm | onboarding-profile-title | - |
| 2 | Chooses the organisation name and slug. | The slug preview updates as the name is typed. | S-035 | organisation name, slug | ERR-ORG-001: The form says the organisation slug is unavailable and suggests another. | unsure | onboarding-org-title | - |
| 3 | Reads the MFA recommendation. | The page explains which roles require MFA and links to enrolment. | S-035 | none | ERR-SYS-001: The system keeps the MFA recommendation visible while the enrolment link is temporarily unavailable. | unsure | onboarding-mfa-note | - |
| 4 | Starts the first domain from the checklist. | The add-domain wizard opens at its first step. | S-035 | domain name | ERR-DOM-001: The domain name is not valid; the IDNA rule is explained. | calm | onboarding-domain-cta | Completion rate of the add-domain wizard |
| 5 | Reads the checklist on the overview. | The checklist lists the remaining tasks and the first-domain call to action. | S-040 | none | ERR-SYS-001: The system keeps the checklist visible while the overview is temporarily unavailable. | calm | onboarding-checklist-title | - |
| 6 | Dismisses the checklist. | The checklist disappears and can be reopened from the overview. | S-040 | dismissed flag | ERR-PERM-001: The page explains that dismissing the checklist needs the owner permission. | calm | onboarding-checklist-dismiss | - |
| 7 | Starts the first domain from the overview. | The add-domain wizard opens and the checklist stays until the domain is active. | S-042 | domain name | ERR-DOM-006: The page says the domain limit for this plan is reached and links to the plan page. | calm | onboarding-first-domain | Time to first domain in monitor mode |
| 8 | Sets the alert email in the wizard. | The wizard keeps the address and states when alerts are sent. | S-040 | alert email | ERR-VAL-001: The form lists the invalid alert email field and keeps the entered values. | calm | onboarding-alert-email | - |

## Trust moments

| Step | What the user must believe | Evidence we show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | The organisation name and slug are mine to choose. | The slug preview names the final URL before it is saved. |
| 5 | The checklist is a guide, not a nag. | The checklist can be dismissed and reopened, and it states which task blocks protection. |

## Security considerations

| Step | Risk | Control | ATK |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | A slug is chosen to impersonate another organisation. | The slug is unique per installation and checked with ERR-ORG-001. | ATK-161 |
| 7 | A crafted request starts a domain that the user may not add. | The add-domain action needs the domain-create permission and a CSRF token. | ATK-172 |

- `AUD-auth.signup` supplies the counter for the account that starts onboarding.
- The two measures named above read the domain events from the audit log; B8 has no onboarding-specific event, and the gap is stated here rather than invented.
