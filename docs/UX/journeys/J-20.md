# J-20 Owner configures rate limits for login

Personas: [Maya](../personas/maya.md), [Dan](../personas/dan.md). Emotional state is a design aid, not a measured value.

Trigger: the owner wants the login path to resist credential stuffing without blocking normal sign-in.

## Steps

| Step | User action | System response | Screen | Data collected | Error path | Emotional state | Copy key | Metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Opens the rate-limit presets. | The page lists the login preset and the current rules. | S-061 | none | ERR-PERM-001: The page lists the permission the role lacks and the login preset stays read-only. | calm | rate-preset-title | - |
| 2 | Chooses the login preset after the preview. | The preview shows the login path, the limit and the progressive ban. | S-061 | preset | ERR-VAL-005: The limit value is outside the allowed range and the range is shown. | unsure | rate-preset-choose | - |
| 3 | Applies the preset to the detected login endpoints. | The page lists the endpoints and the rule that now applies. | S-061 | endpoints | ERR-WAF-004: The saved policy revision conflicts with the preset you applied; reload to see the current login endpoints. | calm | rate-apply | - |
| 4 | Tests the preset with the burst helper. | The helper sends the allowed burst and shows the decision per request. | S-061 | none | ERR-AUTH-004: The helper stops after too many attempts and gives the retry time. | unsure | rate-burst-test | - |
| 5 | Reads the progressive ban ladder. | The page states each step, the TTL and the release time. | S-061 | none | ERR-BAN-004: The ladder says the ban limit is reached and the oldest expiry is evicted. | calm | rate-ban-ladder | - |
| 6 | Enables the rule after the confirmation. | The page states that the limit is active and shows the hit count. | S-061 | none | ERR-PERM-001: The page cannot make the limit active because the role lacks the permission. | calm | rate-enable | - |

## Trust moments

| Step | What the user must believe | Evidence we show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | The preview matches what the preset will do. | The preview states the path, the limit and the ban ladder from the same rule. |
| 4 | The burst test does not attack a real origin. | The helper is labelled and sends its requests only through the inspect API. |

## Security considerations

| Step | Risk | Control | ATK |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | A credential-stuffing burst looks like normal login traffic. | The login preset counts attempts per address and path before the ban. | ATK-001 |
| 4 | The burst helper is mistaken for an attack. | The helper requests carry the acceptance marker and stop at the allowed burst. | ATK-002 |
| 6 | A limit blocks the owner's own login. | The self-lockout preview and the short TTL show the effect before the rule is active. | ATK-003 |

## What could go wrong

| Step | What goes wrong | Recovery action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | The preset is too strict for a busy login page. | The preview shows the limit and the ladder before the preset is applied. |
| 4 | The burst helper trips the limit it is testing. | The helper stops at the allowed burst and reports the decision per request. |
| 6 | The owner's own login is rate limited. | The self-lockout preview names the owner address before the rule is active. |

## Instrumentation

- `AUD-ratelimit.rule_changed` supplies the counter for the applied preset.
- `AUD-ban.created` records each progressive ban with its rule and TTL step.

## Success and checks

Success: the login preset applies to the detected endpoints and the burst test shows the expected decisions. Checks: AC-11.
