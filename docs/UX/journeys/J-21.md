# J-21 Owner configures alerts and webhooks

Personas: [Dan](../personas/dan.md), [Omar](../personas/omar.md). Emotional state is a design aid, not a measured value.

Trigger: the owner wants to hear about a critical attack without watching the monitor all day.

## Steps

| Step | User action | System response | Screen | Data collected | Error path | Emotional state | Copy key | Metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Opens the alert rules. | The page lists each trigger, threshold and channel. | S-076 | none | ERR-PERM-001: The page lists the permission the role lacks and the alert stays read-only. | calm | alert-rules-title | - |

## Trust moments

| Step | What the user must believe | Evidence we show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | The signing secret is shown once and stored safely. | The page never repeats the secret after the create step. |
| 5 | The test delivery is a real delivery. | The attempt appears in the delivery list with its response status. |

## Security considerations

| Step | Risk | Control | ATK |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | A webhook event name is used to inject a header. | The event name is chosen from a fixed list and validated as an enum. | ATK-001 |
| 4 | The signing secret leaks through a delivery log. | The secret is never logged and the response excerpt is redacted. | ATK-002 |
| 6 | An old secret stays valid after rotation. | The rotation window is finite and the old secret is retired when it ends. | ATK-003 |

## Safeguards

| Step | Risky action | Safeguard | Kind | Copy key | Exact copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Choosing the attack and certificate events. | The trigger list shows the event and threshold before the rule is saved. | preview | alert-event-preview | Review the event and the threshold before you save; the alert rule stays off until the channel test passes. |
| 4 | Creating a webhook secret. | The secret is shown once with a copy control and a warning before it is stored. | confirmation | webhook-secret-confirm | Copy the secret now; it is shown once and cannot be read again from this page. |
| 6 | Rotating the signing secret. | The page keeps the old secret valid for a stated window and shows the new one once. | time box | webhook-rotate-confirm | Rotate the secret after the window is stated; the old secret stays valid until the window ends. |
| 8 | Setting the quiet hours. | The page shows the quiet window and the escalation path before the change saves. | preview | alert-quiet-preview | Review the quiet window and the escalation path before you save; alerts outside it still reach the channel. |

## What could go wrong

| Step | What goes wrong | Recovery action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | The webhook URL points at an internal address. | The SSRF guard rejects the URL and names the reason class. |
| 5 | The test fails at the receiver. | The delivery list keeps the attempt and the retry policy. |
| 6 | The old secret is still used by a receiver. | The page states the rotation window and the retirement time. |

- `AUD-webhook.created` records the endpoint and the delivery test result.
