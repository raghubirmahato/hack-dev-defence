# J-26 Owner invites teammates and assigns roles

Personas: [Alex](../personas/alex.md). Emotional state is a design aid, not a measured value.

Trigger: the agency owner hires an analyst and must give her exactly the access she needs.

## Steps

| Step | User action | System response | Screen | Data collected | Error path | Emotional state | Copy key | Metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Opens the team members page and chooses Invite. | The invite form asks for the email address and the role. | S-081 | none | ERR-PERM-001: The invite form is refused and the missing member invite permission is named. | calm | team-invite-title | Invitations sent per org per week |
| 2 | Enters the teammate's email and picks a role. | The role picker lists the role summaries from the role matrix. | S-081 | email address, role | ERR-ORG-003: A pending invitation for this email address exists, and the resend action is offered. | unsure | team-invite-role | - |
| 3 | Sends the invitation. | The invitation appears in the pending list with an expiry date. | S-082 | none | ERR-RATE-001: The invitation rate limit is reached and the reset time is shown. | calm | team-invite-send | Time from invite to accepted membership |
| 4 | Reviews the pending invitations and resends one. | The pending list states that the invite link has expired. | S-082 | none | ERR-AUTH-009: The invitation link has expired and a new link is offered beside the row. | unsure | team-invite-resend | - |
| 5 | Changes a member's role from viewer to analyst. | The role change is refused because the role is not allowed for this action. | S-081 | new role | ERR-ORG-005: The new role is not allowed for this action and the allowed roles are listed. | unsure | team-role-change | - |
| 6 | Removes a member after a confirmation. | The dialog states what the member loses and protects the last owner. | S-081 | none | ERR-ORG-004: An organization must keep at least one owner, so the last owner stays. | anxious | team-member-remove | - |
| 7 | Opens the roles reference to check a permission. | The page lists each role and its permissions read-only. | S-083 | none | ERR-PERM-001: The permission list refuses the view and names the role that lacks the permission. | calm | team-roles-reference | - |
| 8 | Revokes an invitation that was sent to the wrong address. | The pending list marks it revoked after the state changed. | S-082 | none | ERR-CONC-001: The invitation state changed, so reload and retry with the current row. | calm | team-invite-revoke | - |

## Trust moments

| Step | What the user must believe | Evidence we show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | The role I pick grants exactly what it says. | The role description lists the permissions before the invitation is sent. |
| 6 | Removing a member cannot lock the organisation out. | The dialog names the last owner and refuses the removal that would remove it. |

## Security considerations

| Step | Risk | Control | ATK |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | An invitation reveals whether an address has an account. | The resend answer is identical for every address and the invite link is single use. | ATK-161 |
| 5 | A role change escalates a member beyond the intended scope. | The change needs step-up and writes an audit event with the old and new role. | ATK-172 |
| 6 | The last owner is removed by mistake. | The last-owner guard refuses the change and names the remaining owner. | ATK-295 |

## Accessibility considerations

| 1 | The team members heading | The member count and the invite action | Tab reaches the invite button |
| 3 | The invitations heading | The pending count and the expiry date | Tab moves through the invitation rows |
| 5 | The role picker | The current role and the new role | Arrow keys move through the role options |
| 7 | The roles reference heading | The selected role and its permission count | Tab moves through the permission rows |
| 8 | The pending invitation row | The revoked state and the audit note | Enter opens the row actions |

## Safeguards

| Step | Risky action | Safeguard | Kind | Copy key | Exact copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | Changing a member's role. | The picker shows the permissions the new role adds. | preview | team-role-preview | Review the permissions the new role adds before you save; the audit log keeps the previous role. |
| 6 | Removing a member. | The dialog lists what the member loses and keeps the undo path. | confirmation | team-remove-confirm | Check what the member loses before you remove; the history shows who removed the member and when. |
| 8 | Revoking an invitation. | The row states the address and the revocation time before it saves. | undo | team-revoke-confirm | Revoke the invitation and keep the row visible; the address can be invited again. |

## What could go wrong

| Step | What goes wrong | Recovery action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | The invitation goes to the wrong address. | The pending list shows the address and a revoke action before the link is used. |
| 6 | The last owner is removed. | The last-owner guard refuses the change and names the owner that remains. |
| 7 | The role reference is out of date. | The page links to the permission catalog and shows the version of its source. |

## Stress cases

| Case | What changes | What stays usable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| low bandwidth | The member table loads as a plain list without avatars. | The invite and role actions stay reachable. |
| small screen | The member table stacks and the row actions move into a menu. | Every action stays in the tab order. |
| keyboard only | The menu and the dialogs open from the keyboard. | Focus returns to the row after each dialog. |

## Instrumentation

- `AUD-member.invited` records an invitation with the email HMAC and the role.
- `AUD-member.role_changed` records the old and new role of a membership.
- `AUD-member.removed` records the member removal and who performed it.

## Success and checks

Success: the invited teammate joins with exactly the chosen role, the last owner cannot be removed and every change has an audit row. Checks: AC-36.
