# J-05 User resets a forgotten password

Personas: [Maya](../personas/maya.md), [Alex](../personas/alex.md). Emotional state is a design aid, not a measured value.

Trigger: the user cannot sign in and asks for a reset from the login page.

## Steps

| Step | User action | System response | Screen | Data collected | Error path | Emotional state | Copy key | Metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Opens the reset form and types the email. | The form asks only for the address and nothing else. | S-027 | email | ERR-AUTH-004: The form gives a retry time after too many reset attempts. | anxious | forgot-password-title | - |
| 2 | Submits the address. | The page always says "If an account exists, we sent instructions". | S-027 | email | ERR-AUTH-004: The page gives an email address a retry time after too many submission attempts, with neutral wording. | unsure | forgot-password-neutral | Support-worthy errors per 100 sessions |
| 3 | Opens the link from the message. | The confirm page asks for confirmation before the new password form. | S-028 | token | ERR-AUTH-009: The page says the reset link is invalid or expired and offers a new request. | anxious | reset-password-confirm | - |
| 4 | Types a new password twice. | The form shows the policy and the strength meter. | S-028 | new password | ERR-AUTH-010: The form lists the unmet password policy rules and keeps the typed values. | unsure | reset-password-form | - |
| 5 | Submits the new password. | The password changes and every other session is revoked. | S-028 | new password, token | ERR-AUTH-009: The page refuses a used reset link for this password change and offers a new request. | calm | reset-password-success | - |
| 6 | Reads the confirmation. | A notification email states what changed and when. | S-028 | none | ERR-SYS-001: The system keeps the confirmation page readable while the notification service is unavailable. | calm | reset-password-notice | - |
| 7 | Signs in with the new password. | The login form opens without the old password. | S-023 | email, password | ERR-AUTH-002: The login form says the email or password is incorrect for the new password. | calm | forgot-password-back | - |
| 8 | Lands on the organisation overview. | The overview confirms the session is new. | S-040 | none | ERR-SYS-001: The system keeps the session but the overview is temporarily unavailable. | calm | reset-password-signed-in | - |

## Trust moments

| Step | What the user must believe | Evidence we show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | The page does not confirm whether my address exists. | The same sentence appears for any address, and the form does not echo it. |
| 5 | Anyone holding my old session is out. | The audit record lists the revocations, and sign-in is required again. |

## Security considerations

| Step | Risk | Control | ATK |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Account enumeration through the response. | The neutral page and the uniform timing apply to every address. | ATK-161 |
| 3 | A reset token is guessed or replayed. | Tokens are single-use, short-lived and hashed at rest (IA.md token rules). | ATK-162 |
| 5 | The old password keeps working. | The password change revokes every other session. | ATK-164 |

## Instrumentation

- `AUD-auth.password_reset_requested`, `AUD-auth.password_reset_completed` and `AUD-auth.session_revoked` are the audit sources.
- The support-worthy error counter reads `ERR-AUTH-009` and `ERR-AUTH-010` from the error log.
- Counts below five are shown as "not enough data", as MEASURES.md states.

## Success and checks

Success: the password is changed, every other session is revoked and a notification email is sent. Checks: AC-35, AC-46.
