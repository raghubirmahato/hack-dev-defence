# J-06 User accepts a team invite

Personas: [Dan](../personas/dan.md), [Sara](../personas/sara.md), [Alex](../personas/alex.md), [Omar](../personas/omar.md). Emotional state is a design aid, not a measured value.

Trigger: a team owner invites the user to an organisation, and the invitation message arrives.

## Steps

| Step | User action | System response | Screen | Data collected | Error path | Emotional state | Copy key | Metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Opens the invitation message. | The message names the organisation, the role and the expiry. | email | token | ERR-AUTH-009: The message page says the invitation link is invalid or expired. | unsure | invite-email-title | - |
| 2 | Opens the invitation link. | The page repeats the organisation and the role before any action. | S-029 | token | ERR-AUTH-009: The page says the invitation link has expired and offers a new request. | calm | invite-accept-title | - |
| 3 | Creates a new account from the page. | The invited email is fixed in the form and cannot be edited. | S-020 | email, name, password | ERR-AUTH-010: The account form lists the unmet password rules with the invited address fixed. | unsure | invite-signup-title | - |
| 4 | Submits the new account form. | The account is created for the invited address and the invitation is found again. | S-020 | email, token | ERR-VAL-001: The form rejects an invalid field with pointers and keeps the invitation pending. | calm | invite-signup-submit | - |
| 5 | Signs in as an existing user instead. | The login form opens and the invitation stays pending. | S-023 | email, password | ERR-AUTH-002: The login form says the email or password is incorrect while the invitation stays pending. | unsure | invite-login-title | - |
| 6 | Completes the sign-in. | The invitation page resumes with the same organisation and role. | S-023 | none | ERR-AUTH-007: The page says the session expired and resumes the invitation after a new sign-in. | calm | invite-login-submit | - |
| 7 | Accepts the invitation. | The membership is created with exactly the role the invitation named. | S-029 | none | ERR-ORG-005: The page says the role is not allowed for this action and keeps the invitation open. | calm | invite-accept-action | - |
| 8 | Lands in the team list. | The owner is notified by email that the membership was accepted. | S-081 | none | ERR-SYS-001: The system keeps the membership change and the team list is temporarily unavailable. | calm | team-members-title | - |

## Trust moments

| Step | What the user must believe | Evidence we show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | The invitation is for me and for this organisation. | The page names the organisation, the role and the masked invited address. |
| 7 | Joining does not give me more than the role shown. | The accepted role matches the one displayed on S-029, and the owner is notified. |

## Security considerations

| Step | Risk | Control | ATK |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | An invitation token is guessed or replayed. | Tokens are single-use and short-lived, and a used token answers neutrally (IA.md). | ATK-162 |
| 3 | The invited email is swapped for another address. | The form fixes the invited address and rejects a mismatch. | ATK-161 |
| 7 | The accepted role is raised by tampering. | The server checks the role against the inviter's own permissions (ERR-ORG-005). | ATK-294 |

## Accessibility considerations

| Step | Focus target | Announcement | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The message heading | The organisation name and the expiry in the message | The link is reachable by Tab in a plain-text reader |
| 2 | The accept button | The organisation and role as text | Tab reaches accept and decline |
| 3 | The fixed email field | The field is announced as read-only with its value | Tab moves to the editable fields |
| 5 | The password field | The login heading and the error summary | Tab moves through the fields |
| 7 | The accept button again | The membership state change is announced | Enter accepts, Esc leaves without joining |
| 8 | The team list h1 | The organisation name and the member count | Tab reaches the member rows |

## Instrumentation

- `AUD-auth.signup` counts a new account created through an invitation, and `AUD-auth.login_success` covers the existing-user path.
- No dedicated invitation audit event exists in B8; the gap is stated here instead of inventing an id.
- The owner notification is an email, so the counter for it reads the mail queue rather than an audit event.

## Success and checks

Success: the membership is created and the owner is notified. Checks: AC-35, AC-36.
