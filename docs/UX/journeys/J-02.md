# J-02 Visitor signs up and verifies email

Persona: [Maya](../personas/maya.md). Emotional state is a design aid, not a measured value.

Trigger: the visitor decides to create an account, either through open signup or with an invite already in hand.

## Steps

| Step | User action | System response | Screen | Data collected | Error path | Emotional state | Copy key | Metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Types an email, a name and a password. | The strength meter and the breach check give live feedback while typing. | S-020 | email, name, password | ERR-AUTH-010: The form lists each unmet password rule and keeps the typed values. | unsure | signup-form-title | - |
| 2 | Submits the form. | The next page always says "Check your email", whether or not the address already exists. | S-021 | email | ERR-AUTH-004: The page shows a retry time after too many signup attempts before the check-your-email step. | anxious | signup-submit-action | Support-worthy errors per 100 sessions |
| 3 | Waits for the verification message and opens the mailbox. | The message contains one single-use link and states the expiry. | email | email | ERR-AUTH-004: The resend button gives a retry time after too many message attempts to the mailbox. | anxious | signup-check-email-title | - |
| 4 | Opens the verification link. | The confirm page shows a confirm button and the masked address. | S-022 | token | ERR-AUTH-009: The page says the verification link is invalid or expired and offers a resend. | unsure | verify-email-confirm | - |
| 5 | Confirms the address. | The token is consumed once; the account becomes active. | S-022 | token | ERR-AUTH-009: The page says the link has expired, so confirming the address twice is refused neutrally. | calm | verify-email-success | - |
| 6 | Continues after the confirmation. | A signed-in session starts, or the login page is offered. | S-022 | session | ERR-AUTH-007: The session has expired, so the login page is offered with the saved address. | calm | verify-email-signed-in | - |
| 7 | Lands in the onboarding wizard. | The wizard asks for the profile and the organisation name. | S-035 | name, organisation name | ERR-SYS-001: The system keeps the onboarding values and shows that the wizard is temporarily unavailable. | calm | onboarding-wizard-title | Completion rate of the add-domain wizard |
| 8 | Finishes the first onboarding screen. | The account is active and the organisation exists. | S-035 | organisation slug | ERR-ORG-001: The form says the organisation slug is unavailable and suggests another. | calm | onboarding-welcome-title | - |

## Trust moments

| Step | What the user must believe | Evidence we show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | The page will not reveal whether my address already exists. | The same heading and body appear for a new and an existing address. |
| 5 | The link works once and only for me. | The confirm page states the expiry, and the success page does not echo the token. |

## Security considerations

| Step | Risk | Control | ATK |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Credential stuffing with a breached password. | The breach check rejects known passwords and the form rate limit applies. | ATK-158 |
| 2 | Account enumeration through a different response. | The neutral S-021 page is identical in both cases (B7, ERR-AUTH-002 note). | ATK-161 |
| 5 | A verification token is guessed or replayed. | Tokens are single-use and hashed at rest, and the expiry is short (IA.md token rules). | ATK-162 |

## Accessibility considerations

| Step | Focus target | Announcement | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The email field | The field label and the strength meter as text | Tab moves through the three fields |
| 2 | The primary heading | The "Check your email" heading and the neutral wording | Tab reaches the resend button |
| 3 | The mailbox message | The message heading and the single link | Tab reaches the link in the reader |
| 4 | The confirm button | The heading and the button label | Enter confirms, Tab reaches the resend link |
| 7 | The onboarding h1 | The wizard step name | Tab moves through the fields in order |

## Instrumentation

- `AUD-auth.signup` counts account creation, and `AUD-auth.email_verified` counts the confirmed addresses.
- The support-worthy error counter reads `ERR-AUTH-004` and `ERR-AUTH-009` from the error log.
- The completion rate of the add-domain wizard starts counting from the organisation created here.

## Success and checks

Success: the account is `active`, the organisation is created, and an audit event is written. Checks: AC-35, AC-46.
