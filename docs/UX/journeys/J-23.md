## What could go wrong

| Step | What goes wrong | Recovery action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | The address is unbanned while the attack continues. | The ban history shows the release and the rule can be applied again. |
| 5 | The allowlist entry outlives its reason. | The expiry is mandatory and the list shows the remaining days. |
| 7 | The visitor never learns the outcome. | The receipt states the state and the owner's message template is sent. |

## Owner outcomes

The owner resolves an appeal with one of four outcomes:

- Unban the address now and record the reason in the audit log.
- Allowlist the address for N days with an expiry that the page shows before the save.
- Create a scoped exception with an expiry when the rule is a false positive.
- Decline the appeal with a reason and send a neutral message to the visitor.

## Instrumentation

- `AUD-appeal.resolved` records the outcome and the actor.
- `AUD-ban.released` supplies the counter for an unban decision.

## Success and checks

Success: the appeal is resolved with one of the four outcomes and the audit log records it. Checks: AC-43.
