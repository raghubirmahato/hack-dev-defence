# UXD-003: No third-party analytics

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-10-19

Part of the [UX charter](../CHARTER.md) and of [MEASURES.md](../MEASURES.md). Source: rules R-22 and R-25.

## Context

We need to know whether the UX works. The usual route is an analytics script from another company, which would send
visitor data outside the installation, add a third-party origin to a security product, and break the promise that
HDD sends nothing out except through the one audited egress path. Many installations will be a single operator's
computer, where outside analytics would also be meaningless.

## Decision

No analytics script, tracking pixel, session-replay tool or third-party font or widget is shipped in any HDD page.
The experience measures are computed from data the installation already keeps for its own operation (audit log,
error catalog, request counters) and are shown only to the operator of that installation. Small groups are hidden
rather than reported. Operators may ask their users for feedback through their own channels; the product does not do
it for them.

## Consequences

- Measures are limited to what the audit log and counters can answer, so some questions (for example, where users hesitate)
  stay unanswered; usability is also tested with people in the design phase (P03) and in pilots.
- The content security policy forbids third-party script and connect origins; a test checks the shipped policy.
- If a hosted deployment later wants aggregated product analytics across tenants, that needs a new decision record,
  tenant consent, and a privacy review. This record does not authorize it.
