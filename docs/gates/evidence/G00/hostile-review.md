## Accepted with a reason (recorded as residual risk in docs/STRIDE.md)

- Allowlisted host names are trusted to resolve honestly beyond the metadata/link-local/multicast baseline (lab and test hosts legitimately sit on private addresses); owner T-0163.
- The DNS stub resolver has no TCP fallback for truncated answers (fails closed); owner T-0156.
- A and AAAA are queried independently: if one family fails the other family's answers are still validated and used; the client only ever connects to validated addresses.
- `SystemResolver` uses the tokio blocking pool; a slow system DNS server can occupy it. The hardened path (`UdpResolver`, used by `TargetGuard` and the probes) does not.
- Secret scanner: the verbatim specification trees are skipped (ASM-004/011); a `hdd-secret-scan:allow` marker or the word "digest"/"checksum" excuses a line from the entropy rule (explicit, reviewable); allow blocks of claims-lint are not limited in size; phrases wrapped over two lines are not matched.
- `SecretPath` serializes in its redacted form by design; a serde round trip of `Profile` is therefore lossy and nothing round-trips a profile.
- `EnvVarName` remains a heuristic (see the STRIDE row); the owner of the field semantics is the task that reads the variable.
