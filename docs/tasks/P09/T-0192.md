### T-0192 · Dangling record and takeover candidate scanner
`P09` `[SEC][API][OPS]` · deps: T-0178, T-0156 · size: M · owner: sec
**Objective.** Detect records that could be hijacked, using DNS-only analysis.
**Steps.**
1. Implement the scanner job over managed zones: for each CNAME/ALIAS-like record and MX/SRV/NS target outside the zone, resolve the target; flag NXDOMAIN targets, targets in known takeover-prone provider suffix lists (a curated, dated list in repo with provenance), and delegation records (NS) whose target does not respond authoritatively (lame delegation).
2. Never send HTTP requests to third-party hosts (DNS only); fingerprints that need HTTP are explicitly out of scope and documented.
3. Flag HDD-specific dangling references: CNAMEs pointing to `<site-id>.edge.{{PLATFORM_DOMAIN}}` for sites no longer active, and `_acme-challenge` CNAMEs pointing to removed zones.
4. Produce findings with severity, evidence (query results) and remediation; store with timestamps.
5. Schedule weekly scans and scans after DNS changes; emit attention items and optional notifications.
6. Expose findings in S-045 and the posture scan (P27).
7. Add tests with a loopback authoritative server presenting dangling scenarios.
8. Document limitations (false negatives; the list is not exhaustive).
**Deliverables.**
- scanner, findings UI, tests, provenance list
**Acceptance.**
- A1: NXDOMAIN CNAME targets are flagged.
- A2: HDD edge dangling references are detected after a simulated decommission.
- A3: no HTTP request is made to any scanned target (egress log assertion).
- A4: the provider list has provenance and review dates and is validated by `xtask`.
- A5: findings are deduplicated and resolved automatically when fixed.
**Tests.**
- Integration scenarios; unit: classifier; egress log assertion test.
**Notes.** Matrix ATK-244 and subdomain takeover posture checks (AC-32).
