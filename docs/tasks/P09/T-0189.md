### T-0189 · DNSSEC status and DS check UI (S-049)
`P09` `[UI][API]` · deps: T-0161, T-0182 · size: M · owner: dns
**Objective.** Make DNSSEC risk visible and prevent validation outages.
**Steps.**
1. Implement status: zone signed by HDD (not available in Tier 1), parent DS present or absent (from the delegation checker), DNSKEY at the apex, and the resulting risk: "DS present at parent and HDD serves unsigned zone: validating resolvers will fail".
2. Provide step-by-step guidance for safe removal of DS (remove DS at the registrar, wait at least the DS TTL, then change nameservers) with timing computed from observed TTLs, and a checklist the owner can tick off (persisted).
3. Provide the "import signing keys" explanation as a future option (documented as not available) and the alternative: keep DNSSEC with the old provider by using partial/CNAME mode.
4. Add a hard blocker on activation in `full_ns` when DS is present unless acknowledged (links to T-0168).
5. Provide periodic re-check (daily) with alerts if a DS appears after activation.
6. Add the API and readiness check ("DNSSEC consistency").
7. Add accessibility and copy review.
8. Test with simulated parent zones.
**Deliverables.**
- UI and API, tests, docs
**Acceptance.**
- A1: DS present with unsigned HDD zone shows the risk statement and blocks activation until acknowledged.
- A2: the timing computation uses observed TTLs.
- A3: a DS appearing later raises an alert.
- A4: no claim of DNSSEC support is made (claims-lint).
- A5: guidance is complete and testable by a reviewer.
**Tests.**
- Simulated hierarchy integration; unit: timing computation; browser: checklist persistence.
**Notes.** Online signing is Tier 3 (P32, T-0548).
