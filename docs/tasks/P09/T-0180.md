### T-0180 · SPF, DKIM and DMARC helpers and linting
`P09` `[UI][API]` · deps: T-0178 · size: M · owner: dns
**Objective.** Help owners keep email authentication correct and avoid leaking the origin.
**Steps.**
1. Implement SPF parsing and linting: mechanism syntax, lookup count (10 limit), duplicate records, `all` qualifiers, `include` recursion estimates, and the origin-IP leak check (`ip4:`/`ip6:` containing the origin).
2. Implement an SPF builder (provider includes chosen from user input, `ip4/ip6`, `a`, `mx` options) with warnings and a plain-language explanation.
3. Implement DKIM record validation (selector name format, `v=DKIM1; k=rsa; p=...` syntax, key length warnings) and the TXT chunking helper for long keys.
4. Implement DMARC parsing/builder with policy ramp guidance (`p=none` then `quarantine` then `reject`, `rua` addresses must be mailto, external reporting authorization hint), alignment settings, and syntax lint.
5. Implement MTA-STS and TLS-RPT TXT validators (informational helpers).
6. Present results in the record editor and a "Mail health" panel on the DNS page with Pass/Warn/Unknown states, never claiming deliverability.
7. Never modify mail records automatically; propose changes with diffs.
8. Test against a corpus of real-world-like records (valid and tricky).
**Deliverables.**
- linting library, UI panel, builder forms, tests
**Acceptance.**
- A1: SPF with more than 10 lookups is flagged with the count.
- A2: origin IP inside SPF produces the leak warning (feeds T-0158).
- A3: DMARC guidance suggests the ramp and warns against jumping to `reject` without reports.
- A4: TXT chunking round-trips exactly.
- A5: wording avoids deliverability guarantees (claims-lint).
**Tests.**
- Unit/table tests (120 records); proptest on SPF parser; browser tests of the builder.
**Notes.** Referenced by the readiness mail checks and by T-0059 designs.
