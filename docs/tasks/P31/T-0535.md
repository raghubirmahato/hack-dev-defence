### T-0535 · Cutover pack finalization and rehearsal (RUNBOOK-CUTOVER)
`P31` `[DOC][OPS][QA]` · deps: T-0507, T-0529, T-0534 · size: L · owner: ops
**Objective.** Make the apex cutover a rehearsed procedure with objective go/no-go gates and a tested way back.
**Steps.**
1. Finalize `RUNBOOK-CUTOVER.md` per C17.2 using real profile values: pilot results, parity gate items, monitor-first period, cutover steps with exact registrar actions (host objects/glue for in-bailiwick NS names, NS change, DS state), propagation probes, old-provider mirroring and TTL strategy, communications, and both rollback paths with timing; every step lists a verification command and an undo.
2. Implement the **gate evaluator** (`hddctl cutover gates`): evaluates the seven cutover gates (C17.2) from evidence (profile, acceptance results, exposure board, posture scan, supply-chain checks, DS check, AC-23/AC-18, public-surface checks) and prints GO / NO-GO per item with the exact missing evidence and the operator action; accepting a NO-GO requires `--accept-risk <item>=<reason>` recorded in the audit log and the final report.
3. Rehearse the **fast rollback path** on the pilot: `hddctl safe-mode on`, switch affected records to grey in HDD's zone with the documented origin-firewall relaxation and its undo; measure time to effect; record the exact commands.
4. Rehearse the **slow rollback path** in the lab with a simulated registrar (NS restore) and compute bounds from the parent NS TTL; document the operator's registrar-specific steps as a template (the agent never calls registrar APIs).
5. Prepare **monitoring for cutover**: dedicated Grafana dashboard (queries by rcode, answer correctness probes from multiple public resolvers, TLS handshake success, 5xx, origin errors, WAF verdict distribution by mode, ban creation, appeals, challenge rates), external probes, and alert thresholds tightened for the window.
6. Prepare **communication templates** (internal and customer-facing): maintenance notice, cutover started, completed, rollback notice, post-incident summary; schedule recommendations (low-traffic window, staff availability, avoiding holidays).
7. Prepare the **pre-flight checklist for the day** (backup taken, TTLs lowered and aged, certificate pre-issued, secondary DNS live, upstream mitigation contact confirmed, on-call, rollback owner, abort criteria, time limits) and the **abort criteria** (for example validation failures from validating resolvers, mail routing errors, TLS errors above threshold, origin errors).
8. Run a **full dress rehearsal** in the lab (simulated old provider → HDD) with timed steps and a second person/role (operator or reviewer) reading the runbook only; fix ambiguities.
**Deliverables.**
- final `RUNBOOK-CUTOVER.md`, gate evaluator, rehearsal logs, dashboards, templates
**Acceptance.**
- A1: `hddctl cutover gates` reflects evidence accurately and refuses to mark GO with missing evidence.
- A2: both rollback paths are rehearsed with measured timings (or the slow path simulated and labeled so).
- A3: a reader following the runbook alone completes the lab rehearsal without questions that require the author.
- A4: every step has verification and undo (checklist test).
- A5: abort criteria are measurable and wired to dashboards.
**Tests.**
- Lab dress rehearsal, gate evaluator tests, doc checks.
**Notes.** The runbook is executable documentation; it is tested by being executed.
