### T-0532 · Legal and compliance pack (templates, data map, retention schedule, subprocessors)
`P31` `[DOC][HUMAN]` · deps: T-0101, T-0531 · size: M · owner: docs
**Objective.** Provide clearly marked templates and facts the operator's counsel can review, without giving legal advice or claiming compliance.
**Steps.**
1. Produce **templates** (in `legal/`, each starting with "TEMPLATE: operator must review with legal counsel; not legal advice"): Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Acceptable Use Policy (including prohibited uses: attacking others, illegal content, circumventing HDD for abuse), Data Processing Addendum (controller/processor roles for customer request data), Cookie notice (HDD uses only essential cookies on product surfaces; public site cookie-less), Security overview statement, Subprocessors list (generated from configuration: hosting provider, SMTP provider, GeoIP data provider, CT/search services, feeds, backup destination).
2. Produce the **data map** (`docs/DATA-MAP.md`) generated from the data model: every table with data categories (account, usage, security events, DNS, TLS, audit), personal data fields, purposes, retention, access roles, encryption at rest, deletion behavior, cross-border considerations placeholders; link each item to the code that enforces retention and deletion (tests).
3. Produce the **retention schedule** (defaults and plan overrides) and ensure the UI settings (S-086) reflect it; produce the **DPIA template** pre-filled with technical facts (processing of IP addresses and request metadata, redaction, truncation, evidence snippets).
4. Produce a **security controls mapping** document (not a certification): map HDD features and operational practices to common control families (access control, logging, change management, vulnerability management, incident response, backup) to help the operator in audits, with explicit "HDD provides this capability / the operator must do this process" columns.
5. Implement **consent and notice mechanics** already in product: terms acceptance record at signup (version, timestamp), cookie banner not needed if only essential cookies (documented), marketing email opt-in separate from security email, data export/deletion flows (T-0130) linked in the privacy policy template.
6. Provide **abuse and takedown process templates**: abuse report handling SLA text, notice-and-action template, law-enforcement request handling outline (operator decides), transparency report template.
7. Add **automated checks:** subprocessors list matches configured integrations; legal pages show "template" banners until the operator marks them approved in settings (`legal_approved_at`); public signup cannot be enabled (`signup.mode: open`) until legal pages are marked approved (guard in code).
8. Prepare the **operator questions** for counsel and decisions list (jurisdiction, DPO needs, SCCs, breach notification contacts, age restrictions) as a checklist, not answers.
**Deliverables.**
- `legal/` templates, data map, retention schedule, controls mapping, guard checks
**Acceptance.**
- A1: every legal template carries the review banner and unresolved placeholders are enumerated.
- A2: the data map is generated from the schema and fails CI on undocumented tables/columns holding personal data.
- A3: public signup cannot be opened while legal pages are unapproved (test).
- A4: subprocessor list is derived from configuration (test).
- A5: no document claims compliance or certification.
**Tests.**
- Generators, CI guards, claims-lint.
**Notes.** Legal ownership and brand are operator-provided inputs (first response blocking table).
