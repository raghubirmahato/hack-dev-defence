### T-0540 · Final report, handoff and Phase P31 gate G31
`P31` `[DOC][QA]` · deps: T-0539, T-0534, T-0531 · size: M · owner: qa
**Objective.** Deliver the complete, honest picture of what was built, proven, left unverified and what the operator must do next.
**Steps.**
1. Produce the **final report** (`docs/FINAL-REPORT.md`): scope summary (599 tasks in 35 phases), architecture overview, capability matrix with evidence links, acceptance results table (AC-00..AC-58), headline figures (AC-38 matrix, AC-39 detection and ban figures, false-positive baseline, performance numbers with environment tags, DR RTO/RPO), UNVERIFIED list with reasons and operator actions, deviations (`DEVIATIONS.md` DEV-V5-*), security review summary and residual risks, known limitations of signature-based detection and of single-VPS operation, operational readiness status, and legal/compliance status.
2. Produce `HANDOFF.md`: how to operate, where things are, how to run the acceptance tiers, how to rotate keys, how to restore, how to add rules/detectors, contacts and ownership placeholders, open tasks (backlog P32) with priorities and rationale, and the first 30-day operator checklist.
3. Finalize `STATUS.md` and the task index (`INDEX.md`): all 599 tasks with status (done, deferred with reason, backlog), links to gate reports G00..G31, G33 and G34, and the evidence manifest (SHA-256 list of key artifacts).
4. Run the **final verification**: `xtask verify`, the `release` acceptance tier, `claims-audit`, `docs-test`, supply-chain checks, reproducible build check, signature verification of the release artifacts, and the Vuln-Lab exclusion check; attach outputs to the gate report.
5. Verify **no secrets** exist in the repository history or artifacts (secret scanner over git history and evidence bundles) and that `.gitignore` covers profile and secret files.
6. Hold a **retrospective** (written): what worked, failures and root causes (failure budget log), assumptions that proved wrong, suggested spec changes for v5.1, and metrics (tasks, tests, coverage, mutation score, fuzz executions, lines of code).
7. Present the final report to the operator in chat with a concise summary: what is done and proven, what is unverified and why, what the operator must still do, and the recommended next steps.
8. Tag `gate-G31` and `v1.0.0-rc` (or `v1.0.0` if the operator approves), record the final commit hash and artifact checksums.
**Deliverables.**
- `docs/FINAL-REPORT.md`, `HANDOFF.md`, final `STATUS.md`/`INDEX.md`, `docs/gates/G31.md`
**Acceptance.**
- A1: every one of the 599 tasks has a recorded final status with evidence or reason.
- A2: the final verification suite is green and attached.
- A3: no secret findings in history or artifacts.
- A4: the report contains all UNVERIFIED items with operator actions and no claim exceeding evidence.
- A6: for every tier and phase the report states "N of M checks with PASS evidence, K UNVERIFIED"; the word "complete" appears only where N equals M (section 4.1 rule 6).
- A5: tags exist and artifact checksums are recorded.
**Tests.**
- Full verification outputs attached.
**Notes.** The backlog (P32) continues after M12 under the same rules.
