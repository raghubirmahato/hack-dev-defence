### T-0536 · STOP #2: operator package, confirmations and verification of human steps
`P31` `[HUMAN][OPS]` · deps: T-0535, T-0476, T-0405, T-0394 · size: M · owner: ops
**Objective.** Collect and verify everything only the operator can supply or do, without proceeding on assumptions.
**Steps.**
1. Generate the **STOP #2 package** (`docs/STOP2.md` plus an in-product checklist page for the platform owner): the list of required operator actions with exact instructions, expected evidence and how HDD verifies each: registrar preparation (host objects/glue for `ns1/ns2`, NS change window, registrar lock status, DS state and removal if present), origin firewall allowlist and secret header/mTLS applied (lockdown verification, T-0405), external secondary DNS account/zone/TSIG key exchange (AC-21), upstream DDoS provider contract details and activation procedure, volumetric risk acceptance if applicable, DS-check inputs, SMTP details and SPF/DKIM/DMARC publication, brand and legal approval, GeoIP database, inventory/SBOM, status page hosting off-box, external uptime monitor and heartbeat URL, backup destination and escrow of keys, on-call contacts, maintenance window.
2. Present the package to the operator in chat with a plain-language summary of why each item matters and what remains UNVERIFIED if it is skipped; wait for replies; never mark an item satisfied without evidence (probe results, screenshots supplied by the operator, DNS observations, command output pasted).
3. Implement **automatic verification probes** per item where possible (DNS delegation observed, glue present at the parent, DS state observed, lockdown probe from the runner, secondary answering and transfers working, SPF/DKIM/DMARC records present, heartbeat received, status page reachable externally, backup destination write test) and record results in `docs/OPERATOR-CONFIRMATIONS.md` with timestamps and evidence references.
4. Re-evaluate the **cutover gates** with the new evidence (`hddctl cutover gates`), list NO-GO items, and present the operator with options (provide missing item, accept risk in writing for that specific item, defer cutover).
5. Record **risk acceptances** exactly as given by the operator (text, date), scoped to specific items; broad statements like "accept all risks" are not accepted: ask for item-specific confirmation.
6. Confirm the **cutover window and roles** (who executes registrar changes, who monitors, who can authorize rollback), the **rollback decision deadline**, and communication plan.
7. Produce the **go/no-go brief** summarizing gate states, residual risks and the proposed timeline, and wait for the operator's written decision.
8. If the operator defers, document the deferral state (what remains valid, expiry of evidence such as pre-issued certificates and aged TTLs) and stop; do not perform production-affecting actions.
**Deliverables.**
- STOP #2 package, confirmation log, gate evaluation, go/no-go brief
**Acceptance.**
- A1: every item has evidence or is explicitly listed as unsatisfied; none is assumed satisfied.
- A2: risk acceptances are item-specific and recorded verbatim with dates.
- A3: the gate evaluator output matches the confirmation log.
- A4: the go/no-go brief is delivered and the agent waits for a written decision.
- A5: on deferral no production-affecting action occurs (audit check).
**Tests.**
- Probe tests against lab fixtures; log consistency checks.
**Notes.** STOP #2 is a hard stop: the agent resumes only after the operator replies.
