### T-0136 · Identity gate G06
`P06` `[QA][DOC]` · deps: T-0133, T-0134, T-0135 · size: M · owner: qa
**Objective.** Certify the identity system and the tenant isolation foundation.
**Steps.**
1. Run `cargo xtask verify`, the identity e2e suite, abuse suite, BOLA suite, matrix test and route checks.
2. On the pilot VPS (after M5) or the local stack (before), execute AC-01 and AC-35 subsets and the AC-46 mail checks (SPF/DKIM/DMARC preflight, header safety) with the operator's mailbox only.
3. Collect evidence: statistical test outputs, timing distributions, throttle thresholds observed, email samples, axe summaries, and performance numbers.
4. Verify that platform roles require MFA and that the admin host rejects tenant sessions.
5. Verify audit chain verification passes after the full run.
6. Complete `docs/gates/G06.md` per Appendix E5; list UNVERIFIED items (for example live SMTP until supplied).
7. Update the capability matrix (REQ-IDN rows) and STATUS.
8. Tag `gate-G06`.
**Deliverables.**
- `docs/gates/G06.md`, evidence files
**Acceptance.**
- A1: AC-35, AC-36 (identity part) and AC-46 pass or are UNVERIFIED with reasons.
- A2: no open serious findings from the abuse and security reviews.
- A3: `hddctl audit verify` passes.
- A4: the matrix and BOLA suites show full route coverage.
- A5: the tag exists and the gate report is complete.
**Tests.**
- The full verification outputs with SHA-256 are attached.
**Notes.** After G06, any new route must come with permission annotations and BOLA coverage automatically.
