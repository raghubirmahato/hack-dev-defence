### T-0122 · Login anomaly detection and notifications
`P06` `[SEC]` · deps: T-0121, T-0110 · size: M · owner: sec
**Objective.** Notify users of suspicious sign-ins without alert fatigue.
**Steps.**
1. Define signals: new device (no matching device cookie), new country, impossible travel (distance/time threshold using coarse GeoIP), unusual hour (opt-in), many failures before success, login from Tor/datacenter ASN (policy), first login after long inactivity.
2. Compute a risk level (low, medium, high); low sends nothing; medium sends a notice email once per device per 24 h; high additionally requires step-up or email confirmation depending on policy.
3. Implement user preferences for notifications and a "trusted locations" list (country-level).
4. Implement the post-login interstitial for high risk with options (this was me, secure my account).
5. Implement rate limiting of notices (max 3 per day per user) to avoid being an email bomb vector.
6. Implement admin-visible aggregate metrics (rates of anomalies) without personal details.
7. Add audit events and timeline entries.
8. Test with scripted travel scenarios using the test clock and a controllable GeoIP source (a real MaxMind-format fixture database built in tests).
**Deliverables.**
- risk module, templates, tests
**Acceptance.**
- A1: a login from a new country triggers one notice and not repeated notices.
- A2: impossible travel is flagged with documented thresholds.
- A3: notices are capped per day.
- A4: GeoIP absence disables geo signals without errors.
- A5: users can opt out of medium-risk notices but not high-risk or security-critical ones.
**Tests.**
- Unit: distance/time function; scenario tests for the risk engine; integration: email counts.
**Notes.** The risk engine's logic is transparent in the docs (no opaque scoring).
