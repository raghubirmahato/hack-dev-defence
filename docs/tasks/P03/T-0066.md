### T-0066 · Reports, notifications, integrations and webhooks (S-073 to S-079)
`P03` `[UI]` · deps: T-0057 · size: M · owner: ux
**Objective.** Design reporting and integration screens.
**Steps.**
1. Design Reports (S-073) and Report builder/schedule (S-074): templates, period, domains, sections, recipients, schedule, preview, run history, download.
2. Design the Notification center (S-075) with severity filters and bulk read.
3. Design Alert rules (S-076): triggers with thresholds, channel routing, quiet hours, throttle explanation.
4. Design Integrations (S-077): cards per channel with setup steps and test button.
5. Design Webhooks (S-078) and deliveries (S-079): secret reveal once, signing explanation with verification code sample, retry policy, redeliver.
6. Design failure states: bounced email, channel disabled after failures, SSRF-blocked URL message.
7. Design empty states and permission states.
8. Provide small-screen variants.
**Deliverables.**
- prototype pages and states
**Acceptance.**
- A1: the webhook secret is shown exactly once in the design with a copy action and warning.
- A2: delivery status uses text plus icon.
- A3: alert rules show the resulting notification preview.
- A4: report builder previews sections and honest measurement notes.
- A5: SSRF-blocked messages do not leak internal details.
**Tests.**
- Axe; heuristic review.
**Notes.** Signing code samples show verification in a generic language-agnostic way plus Rust and curl.
