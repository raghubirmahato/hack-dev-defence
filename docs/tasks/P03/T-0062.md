### T-0062 · Rules, rule builder, dry-run and exceptions (S-058 to S-060, S-064)
`P03` `[UI]` · deps: T-0049, T-0061 · size: L · owner: ux
**Objective.** Design rule management for analysts and developers.
**Steps.**
1. Design the Rules list (S-058): priority ordering with move up/down, enable toggles, hits, expiry, action pills, status (active, expired, rolled back), bulk actions.
2. Design the Rule builder (S-059) per B12.2: header fields, condition groups, action, preview sentence, sample tester, self-lockout warning, save with revision note.
3. Design Rule dry-run (S-060): replay summary, false-positive candidates with evidence, known-good probes results, recommendation text.
4. Design Exceptions (S-064): scope definition (rule, path, parameter, method), mandatory reason and expiry, hits, creation from an event.
5. Design the diff view for rule changes and links to revisions.
6. Define the error taxonomy messages for regex subset violations with examples of supported alternatives.
7. Provide states: invalid expression, too complex, pack-provided read-only rules, expired rules.
8. Design import/export of rule sets (JSON) with validation preview.
**Deliverables.**
- prototype pages and states
**Acceptance.**
- A1: rules reorder without drag.
- A2: unsupported regex constructs produce actionable messages in the design.
- A3: exceptions require reason and expiry in the design and copy.
- A4: dry-run shows both matched traffic and known-good probe outcomes.
- A5: read-only platform-pack rules are distinguishable and explain why.
**Tests.**
- Axe; keyboard walkthrough; heuristic review.
**Notes.** Platform-published pack rules cannot be edited by tenants, only overridden by scoped exceptions.
