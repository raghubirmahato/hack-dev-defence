### T-0059 · Domain overview, readiness and DNS screens (S-043 to S-049, S-099)
`P03` `[UI]` · deps: T-0057 · size: L · owner: ux
**Objective.** Design the domain home and DNS management experience.
**Steps.**
1. Design the domain header (status, WAF mode, readiness) and Overview tab: traffic and threat charts, recent events, next-best-action card, quick actions.
2. Design Readiness (S-044): ring, grouped checklist, remediation links, history.
3. Design DNS records (S-045) with filters, inline edit, bulk, proxied toggles, TTL presets; the record editor (S-046) per type with validation and "what will resolve" preview.
4. Design Import and parity (S-047), History and export (S-048), DNSSEC and DS check (S-049), Propagation checker (S-099).
5. Define conflict and safety messages (CNAME conflicts, mail record proxy attempt, deleting required records) with exact copy.
6. Design the CloudToggle consequence popover content.
7. Design partial-mode domains (no DNS tab content except records HDD controls) with explanation.
8. Provide state variants (no records, unsupported types, propagation pending, errors).
**Deliverables.**
- prototype pages and states
**Acceptance.**
- A1: every destructive DNS action has preview and undo (via history).
- A2: the record editor shows type-specific help and validation for A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT, NS, SRV, CAA, HTTPS.
- A3: the proxied toggle conveys state with text and icon.
- A4: partial-mode screens explain limits honestly.
- A5: DS-record warning content exists with next steps.
**Tests.**
- Axe; heuristic review; copy review.
**Notes.** Mail DNS safety (SPF/DKIM/DMARC helpers) is specified in T-0180.
