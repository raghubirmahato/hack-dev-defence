### T-0407 · Continuous origin-exposure monitoring and re-IP guidance
`P21` `[API][SEC][OPS]` · deps: T-0165, T-0405, T-0201 · size: M · owner: sec
**Objective.** Notice when the origin address leaks after onboarding and tell the owner what to do.
**Steps.**
1. Schedule the analysis of T-0165 as a continuous job (daily plus on any DNS or certificate event) with diffing against the previous result, producing `origin_exposure_findings` with stable ids, first_seen, last_seen and status.
2. Add data sources (each optional, each honest about availability): zone records in HDD's own zone (new A/AAAA/MX/TXT/SPF include equal to or near the origin), CT-log observations for names resolving to the origin (T-0201), SPF `ip4:`/`ip6:` entries and DMARC reports addressed to the origin, outbound email header samples uploaded by the owner (analysis happens in the browser or server-side with no storage beyond the extracted IPs), and response headers from the origin captured by the connectivity test.
3. Implement an **"IP-on-certificate" check**: when the origin presents a certificate for the site name directly on its IP (observed by the connectivity probe), flag that internet-wide certificate scanners can index the origin; recommend serving a default certificate for non-matching SNI at the origin.
4. Implement the **re-IP assistant**: a guided checklist (new IP provisioning, copy config, update origin in HDD with a staged switch, lock down, retire the old IP) that integrates with the origin pool concept (single origin in Tier 1; the staged switch is a two-step change with health verification and rollback).
5. Notify owners when findings appear (attention item, email per preferences) with severity (exposed with evidence, possible exposure, informational) and never alarm on absence of evidence.
6. Add platform-level metrics for exposure categories (counts only) for the admin panel.
7. Expose API endpoints (`GET D/origin/exposure`, `POST D/origin/exposure/{id}:acknowledge` with reason) and integrate into the Protection Readiness Score as a reversible penalty.
8. Write tests with scenario fixtures: MX pointing at origin, SPF containing the origin IP, a wildcard certificate observation, a record added later, ack/unack lifecycle, and false-positive guards (shared hosting IPs where the origin legitimately shares an address; marked as `shared_address_hint`).
**Deliverables.**
- job, findings model, UI cards, API, tests
**Acceptance.**
- A1: adding an A record equal to the origin IP creates a finding within one job run.
- A2: findings carry evidence and a remediation link; absence of data never yields a "not exposed" claim.
- A3: acknowledgements require a reason and expire in 90 days.
- A4: the shared-address hint prevents alarm fatigue in the fixtures.
- A5: no outbound request goes to a service that is not in the egress allowlist.
**Tests.**
- Scenario tests (20), job idempotency, RLS, wording lint.
**Notes.** Ties to ATK-244 and the subdomain/takeover checks of `hdd-scan`.
