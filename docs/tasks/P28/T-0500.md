### T-0500 · AC-34, AC-08 and AC-51: ban poisoning resistance, persistence, global jail and never-ban
`P28` `[QA][SEC]` · deps: T-0332, T-0463, T-0491 · size: L · owner: qa
**Objective.** Prove that bans are durable, correct, hard to abuse and safe for legitimate users.
**Steps.**
1. **AC-34 (poisoning resistance):** attempt to get a victim IP banned via (a) cross-site subresource requests with attack payloads from pages the victim visits (CSRF-style forced requests using Fetch Metadata `cross-site` headers; expect 403 without ban), (b) spoofed `X-Forwarded-For`/`Forwarded`/`X-Real-IP` headers (expect real IP banned, spoofed value ignored), (c) IPv6 /64 rotation by the attacker (single /64 ban key), (d) shared-address (CGNAT) mitigations: many benign users behind one IP with one attacker (ban converts to challenge/rate-limit ladder per policy, documented outcome), (e) honeypot hits through cross-site context, (f) soft-score accumulation attempts without hard hits, (g) bans triggered against never-ban addresses (expect refusal and `never_ban_attack_attempt` event), (h) mass-ban anomaly (poisoning) alerts.
2. **AC-08 (persistence and unban):** ban → verify at L7, Redis, Postgres, kernel set (if present); restart proxy, flush Redis, restart Postgres in sequence and confirm the ban persists and is re-enforced; unban via UI/API/CLI ≤ 2 s everywhere; TTL expiry; ban listing exports; run with IPv4 and IPv6; test the local enforcement copy keeps working with Redis down.
3. **AC-51 (global jail and never-ban):** promotion after 3 distinct orgs within 1 h (lab orgs), global jail enforcement across tenants, manual global ban/unban, never-ban collisions and runner guard, review queue, propagation acknowledgments (proxy/firewall/XDP).
4. Verify **appeal-related** behavior with PoW (T-0324 flows) at a smoke level (full AC-43 in T-0503).
5. Verify **self-lockout guard:** an owner attempting to ban or block their own IP/CIDR through bans, access lists and rules gets the preview warning and a refusal unless confirmed with a break-glass path; the admin CIDRs are never blocked.
6. Verify **timing and accuracy:** ban creation to enforcement p95 ≤ 2 s at L7 and ≤ 2 s at kernel (pilot with agent), false-ban rate for legitimate corpora (0).
7. Capture evidence and update status lines.
**Deliverables.**
- evidence bundle with scenario table
**Acceptance.**
- A1: no poisoning scenario results in a ban of a victim address (all tested vectors).
- A2: bans persist across the restart/flush sequence and unban is effective ≤ 2 s.
- A3: global jail promotion and never-ban behaviors match the spec in the lab multi-org scenario.
- A4: the runner IP cannot be banned or removed from never-ban during the run.
- A5: self-lockout prevention works for bans, access lists and rules.
**Tests.**
- Lab multi-org scenarios and pilot sandbox checks.
**Notes.** Cross-site subresource context rules are in C6.5; this task verifies them adversarially.
