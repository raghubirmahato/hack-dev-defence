### T-0501 · AC-11, AC-44 and AC-48: rate limiting, challenge and bots, scanner detection (with lab oracles)
`P28` `[QA][LAB]` · deps: T-0351, T-0496, T-0491 · size: L · owner: qa
**Objective.** Verify behavioral defenses against real tools and traffic shapes, in the lab where tools are permitted.
**Steps.**
1. **AC-11 (rate limiting):** per-IP, per-path and auth-endpoint limits verified with `hdd-loadgen` at the correct profiles on the sandbox (live) and heavily in the lab: sliding window accuracy (±5%), 429 pages with `Retry-After`, progressive penalties and auto-ban thresholds, Redis-down local fallback, per-tenant fairness (noisy tenant does not starve others), IPv6 /64 aggregation, distributed attack detection (many IPs one endpoint → elevated protection V16).
2. **AC-44 (challenge and bots):** challenge issuance, proof-of-work solving by a real headless browser and by a non-JS client (fallback path), clearance cookie scope and replay protection, under-attack mode behavior for new clients, verified-bot FCrDNS (forward-confirmed reverse DNS) positive and negative cases with simulated resolvers in the lab, bot scoring classes against crafted client profiles (curl, python-requests, headless Chrome with and without stealth patches, real Chrome), accessibility of the challenge page (S-141/S-147) and API client bypass rules.
3. **AC-48 (scanner/bot detection):** run `nikto`, `wapiti`, `nuclei`, `ffuf`, `OWASP ZAP baseline`, `sqlmap` and `slowhttptest` against the Vuln-Lab through HDD (lab only) and record: detection by fingerprint (user-agent and behavioral), bans issued, requests passed before detection, forced-browsing detector results, honeypot hits, and the effect on the oracles' own findings (what they still manage to report); live check limited to user-agent fingerprints on the sandbox (Class A).
4. **Credential stuffing lab:** synthetic credential lists (generated, no real data) against the Vuln-Lab login through HDD with rotating source IPs (lab namespaces), verifying detection signals (failure ratio, HMAC'd username diversity, success-after-failures) and the strict auth-endpoint mode; measure how many attempts succeed before mitigation and document the figures.
5. **Slow attacks:** slowloris/slow POST/RUDY-style behaviors via `slowhttptest` in the lab: timeouts, connection caps and per-IP limits keep legitimate clients served.
6. **False-positive checks:** legitimate high-rate clients (API consumers with keys, monitoring services) with exceptions, mobile apps sharing CGNAT addresses, and search-engine crawlers (verified) remain unaffected.
7. Record exact figures and un-mitigated gaps; open diagnosis tasks for any miss; update the attack matrix status for ATK-IDs covered.
8. Archive evidence; update AC-11/AC-44/AC-48 status.
**Deliverables.**
- evidence bundle with oracle outputs, figures, gap list
**Acceptance.**
- A1: AC-11 accuracy and fallback criteria met.
- A2: the challenge works for browsers and has a documented non-JS path; bypassing with plain curl is not possible when enforced.
- A3: scanner tools are detected and banned within the stated request budget (report numbers).
- A4: legitimate clients in the FP scenarios are not blocked.
- A5: credential stuffing mitigation figures are recorded with limits stated.
**Tests.**
- Lab runs with oracles; sandbox live subset.
**Notes.** Oracles run only in `lab` mode against Vuln-Lab addresses.
