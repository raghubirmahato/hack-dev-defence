### T-0379 · Certificate Transparency monitoring wiring and issuance verification
`P19` `[OPS][SEC]` · deps: T-0201, T-0376 · size: S · owner: sec
**Objective.** Close the loop between issuance and CT observation.
**Steps.**
1. After each issuance, record the serial and expected SCT/CT presence; schedule CT queries to confirm the entry appears within the CA's expected delay (for staging, the staging log if available).
2. Implement the platform-level CT watcher for `{{PLATFORM_DOMAIN}}` and its subdomains (admin, app, api) as high-priority watches with immediate alerts to platform admins on unexpected issuance.
3. Implement tenant opt-in alerts (default on for verified domains).
4. Implement the expected-issuer allowlist (our ACME CA and the owner's uploaded issuers).
5. Implement dedupe and rate limits, with graceful degradation when the CT service is unavailable.
6. Surface in S-051 and the platform dashboard.
7. Test with a local CT-search-like service.
8. Document limitations.
**Deliverables.**
- wiring, tests
**Acceptance.**
- A1: unexpected issuer for a platform hostname triggers a critical alert within one poll interval.
- A2: expected issuance is marked confirmed when found.
- A3: CT service outage does not break issuance flows.
- A4: tenant alerts are deduplicated.
- A5: the staging CT behavior is documented (AC-04).
**Tests.**
- Integration with local service; unit: expected matching.
**Notes.** Requires a CT search service in the egress allowlist; otherwise `UNVERIFIED(capability_not_supplied)`.
