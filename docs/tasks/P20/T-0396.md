### T-0396 · ACME DNS-01 serving, CNAME-delegated challenges and secondary-aware waiting
`P20` `[DP][SEC]` · deps: T-0390, T-0394, T-0376 · size: L · owner: dns
**Objective.** Make DNS-01 validation work for full-NS, hidden-primary and CNAME-delegated cases with correct timing.
**Steps.**
1. Implement the challenge TXT store as a separate, small, in-memory structure inside the snapshot service fed by `acme_challenges` revisions (zone id, name, token digest, expiry), so challenge records can be published and removed without bumping the customer-visible zone serial and without creating customer-visible version history entries (they are tagged `source=acme`, hidden in the UI except in the certificate diagnostics view).
2. Serve `_acme-challenge.<name>` TXT sets for zones HDD is authoritative for, and for names that CNAME to `<zone-id>.acme.<platform_domain>`: the synthetic acme zone answers TXT with the live challenge values for the tenant's zone only; verify the CNAME chain resolution through the engine (no wildcard abuse).
3. Implement cleanup: challenge records expire at the earlier of their TTL (60 s) and explicit removal after validation or failure; hard maximum lifetime 30 minutes; at most 20 live challenge values per name (wildcard + apex pairs need two).
4. Implement the **secondary-aware wait**: in `hidden_primary` topology the ACME worker must not request validation until (a) NOTIFY was acknowledged, (b) the secondary returns the TXT for the exact value (query the secondary's addresses directly from the control plane worker), and (c) a configurable propagation delay passed; a timeout (default 5 minutes) fails the order with `ERR-ACME-003` (then `ERR-ACME-002` if validation is finally refused) and a clear explanation visible in S-051; the other order failures use `ERR-ACME-001`, `-004`, `-005`, `-006` and `-007` from B7.
5. Implement authoritative-consistency self-checks before validation: the worker queries every HDD NS address for the TXT and requires all to answer identically.
6. Implement `CAA` enforcement checks at the DNS level for the platform's own names and reflect CAA records of tenant zones into the answers (T-0183 UI) with `issue "letsencrypt.org"` suggestions governed by the ACME settings.
7. Protect against challenge-record abuse: tenants cannot create `_acme-challenge` TXT records with arbitrary content in a way that blocks the platform's own validation (platform-owned challenge rows take precedence; duplicates are merged; a tenant record at a name used by the platform is shown in the UI with a warning).
8. Add tests with a real Pebble or Boulder-staging-compatible test CA (lab oracle `pebble`, UNVERIFIED(`oracle_unavailable`) if absent) for the three paths: direct, hidden-primary, CNAME-delegated, and a negative test where the secondary is slow.
**Deliverables.**
- ACME challenge store and serving, worker wait logic, docs, tests
**Acceptance.**
- A1: a wildcard plus apex DNS-01 order completes against the test CA in all three paths.
- A2: validation is never requested before the secondary serves the TXT (instrumented test).
- A3: challenge records do not appear in zone version history or bump the visible serial.
- A4: a slow secondary produces a clear, actionable failure, not a CA-side failed-validation count (budget preserved).
- A5: stale challenge TXT records are gone within 30 minutes.
**Tests.**
- Integration with Pebble; timing tests with a delayed fake secondary.
**Notes.** Challenge TXT records do change the data a secondary must serve; the secondary-aware wait is the guard that prevents wasting CA failed-validation budget (C14.3).
