### T-0402 · DNS live acceptance: AC-03 (full), AC-21 and AC-28 on the pilot and the closed lab
`P20` `[QA][HUMAN]` · deps: T-0394, T-0395, T-0396, T-0398, T-0401, T-0218 · size: L · owner: qa
**Objective.** Execute the acceptance criteria that depend on the live DNS service and prepare the evidence that the cutover gate will later rely on.
**Steps.**
1. Execute AC-03 on the pilot subzone: A/AAAA/CNAME/MX/TXT/CAA/SRV/HTTPS answers, wildcard and ENT behavior, `ANY` minimization, NXDOMAIN/NODATA with SOA, REFUSED for foreign zones, EDNS and truncation, TCP, case preservation, proxied versus grey answers, parity report for the imported pilot zone, TTL behavior, low-TTL policy, and propagation measured from public resolvers.
2. Execute AC-21 in both topologies: (a) `self_authoritative` with a TSIG-fed external secondary if the operator supplied one (UNVERIFIED(`capability_not_supplied`) otherwise, with a lab BIND/Knot standing in only for the protocol path), (b) `hidden_primary` where the secondary answers publicly and HDD refuses public sources. Verify NOTIFY/IXFR lag, SOA serial match, failover behavior when the HDD process is stopped (the secondary keeps answering within the SOA expire window), and recovery.
3. Execute AC-28 in the closed lab: amplification ratios (T-0398), RRL under spoofed sources, TC=1 and TCP fallback, DNS cookies, random-subdomain defense, reflection-port guard, and AXFR refusal without TSIG.
4. Verify the survivability claims precisely: record which of the three statements hold with evidence (HDD stops, secondary serves), and which cannot be verified (registrar-level failures, provider anycast capacity) as UNVERIFIED with reasons; update the capability matrix (REQ-DNS) and the DDoS readiness document (`docs/DDOS-READINESS.md`) wording to match the evidence (R-10).
5. Test the failover mechanics prep for L6 (manual swap of proxied answers to `standby_edge_ips`): dry-run in the pilot, measure time to effect across public resolvers with a low TTL, and document the expected delay.
6. Emit STOP-style instructions (T-0216) for any human step needed (secondary account, NS records at the secondary, TSIG key exchange, registrar host objects); the suite continues with the checks that do not depend on them.
7. Archive evidence with SHA-256, update the AC-03/AC-21/AC-28 status lines in `STATUS.md` and the acceptance report.
8. Review the results with a hostile mindset: attempt unauthorized AXFR from non-allowlisted addresses, from the secondary's address without TSIG, and with a stale TSIG time; attempt zone enumeration via NSEC-like probing (should reveal nothing).
**Deliverables.**
- AC-03/21/28 evidence bundle, updated capability matrix and DDoS readiness doc, human-step instructions
**Acceptance.**
- A1: AC-03 PASS on the pilot (all rows) with evidence.
- A2: AC-21 PASS or UNVERIFIED with exact reasons (`capability_not_supplied`, `human_step_pending`) for the secondary-dependent rows only.
- A3: AC-28 PASS in the closed lab; no live-internet flood was generated (audit log check).
- A4: capability matrix wording matches the evidence (claims-lint passes).
- A5: failover dry-run timings are documented with measured numbers.
**Tests.**
- Live runs from the runner under the correct rate profiles; lab runs in namespaces.
**Notes.** The external secondary is a required cutover dependency (C17.2 gate 2); this task proves the mechanism so the operator's secondary choice is the only remaining unknown.
