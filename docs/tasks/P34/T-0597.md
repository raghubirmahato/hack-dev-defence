### T-0597 · Parser-differential rejection and inspection-evasion defenses
`P34` `[DP][SEC]` · deps: T-0249, T-0257, T-0360, T-0302 · size: L · owner: waf
**Objective.** Reduce the effect of representation tricks and padding on inspection, for the variants listed in the matrix.
**Steps.**
1. Implement strict-parse policies with explicit outcomes: duplicate JSON keys, comments, trailing commas, BOM, lone surrogates and numbers beyond safe ranges (reject, canonicalize or pass as a string, by per-field site policy, so large identifiers above 2^53 are not broken), multipart edge cases (duplicate part names, malformed boundaries, `filename*`, LF-only lines, nested multiparts), and re-serialize-and-compare used for comparison only (forwarded bytes are never changed unless the owner opts in, so signed webhooks keep verifying). Every strict policy ships in `monitor` first with a would-have-rejected counter; LF-only multipart and duplicate keys are allowed per site where observed legitimate clients need them.
2. Implement charset and encoding confusion defenses: a per-site charset allowlist seeded from the charsets observed in passive data (T-0486; `utf-8`, `iso-8859-1`, `windows-1252` as the minimum, with Shift_JIS, GBK, EUC-KR, Windows-1251 and ISO-8859-2 available as presets), rejection of UTF-7, UTF-16 without BOM agreement, `ibm037`, `ibm500` and other EBCDIC charsets, and decode-compare between declared and detected encoding.
3. Implement content-type switching defenses: per-endpoint content-type policy from learned or declared schemas (T-0302, T-0576) and rejection of a body whose shape contradicts its type.
4. Extend the tail-window and field-wise inspection of T-0257 with **rolling-window inspection** beyond the inspect prefix for suspicious shapes (padding detection by entropy and repeated-junk ratio) with a bounded budget, which narrows the room to hide a payload behind padding; `over_limit_policy` remains the backstop and, under the default `inspect_prefix`, the verdict is tagged `truncated`.
5. Implement reassembly before detection for payloads split across chunks, multipart parts, parameters and cookies (ATK-353), with bounds.
6. Implement HTTP/2 pseudo-header, CRLF-in-field and authority consistency checks (ATK-343, ATK-354) and WebSocket upgrade validation (ATK-355) if not already complete.
7. Add differential fuzzing between HDD's parsers and reference parsers of popular stacks for each format.
8. Add metrics, events and documentation of every rejection reason.
**Deliverables.**
- strict parsers, charset and type defenses, rolling-window inspection, reassembly, differential fuzzing, tests
**Acceptance.**
- A1: every row ATK-348 to ATK-355 has fixtures that are rejected or neutralized, with no false positive on the benign corpora and on legitimate-client fixtures (non-UTF-8 charsets, large identifiers, signed webhook bodies, LF-only clients).
- A2: a 1 MiB padded request hiding a Class A SQLi shape after the inspect prefix is detected in `block` mode with the rolling window, is blocked under `over_limit_policy=block`, and is tagged `truncated` under the default policy.
- A3: payloads split across chunks, parts and parameters are detected after reassembly (matrix test).
- A4: the differential fuzzer runs 10 minutes per format in `verify` without a disagreement that is not listed as a documented policy.
- A5: the added work stays within the E4 inspection budgets (benchmark).
**Tests.**
- Fixture matrix; differential fuzz; benchmarks.
**Notes.** Rejecting ambiguous input is the safest answer to a parser differential; policies can allow canonicalization where an owner needs it.
