### T-0007 · Deliver the first response and seed the open-questions register
`P00` `[DOC][HUMAN]` · deps: T-0003, T-0006 · size: S · owner: docs
**Objective.** Execute the first-response protocol (section 9) so the operator answers what blocks later work, then wait or proceed.
**Steps.**
1. Restate the mission and safety envelope in at most 15 lines (sections 1 and 2).
2. Run `hddctl preflight --profile-only` and paste the table; if `hdd.live.yaml` is missing, list every required field with how to provide it.
3. Paste the environment-discovery summary from `docs/ENV.md` with degradations.
4. Present the P00 to P03 plan with acceptance criteria and an honest effort estimate in sessions, not dates.
5. List the human-gated steps and when they occur (M1 design sign-off, M3 SMTP, M5 STOP #1, M12 STOP #2, production ACME, `load`, cutover).
6. Ask the DDoS-readiness questions (C11.2), the inventory questions (C12.2) and the product questions of Appendix E8, grouped in one message, each with what it blocks.
7. Record every unanswered item in `STATUS.md` Open questions with the blocked task IDs.
8. If any **required** profile field is missing, stop and wait; otherwise continue with T-0008.
**Deliverables.**
- The first response message (kept in `docs/FIRST-RESPONSE.md`)
- `STATUS.md` open-questions section populated
**Acceptance.**
- A1: the message contains exactly the six items of section 9 and no code.
- A2: every question has a blocked-task list.
- A3: the table of blocking dependencies is included.
- A4: the register distinguishes "blocks pilot" from "blocks cutover".
- A5: nothing in the message includes secrets or full file contents.
**Tests.**
- Review checklist: compare the message with section 9 item by item (recorded in the commit body).
**Notes.** Questions are batched; later questions go into the register, not into new interruptions (section 0.5).
