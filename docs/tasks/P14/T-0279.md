### T-0279 · SQL-prose and structured benign suite
`P14` `[QA]` · deps: T-0255, T-0278 · size: M · owner: qa
**Objective.** Make sure natural language about databases and tricky legitimate input never triggers.
**Steps.**
1. Extend `benign-sql-prose.txt` to at least 600 entries focusing on adversarial benign inputs: questions from developer forums, tutorial sentences, error messages quoted by users, product descriptions with "union", "select", "drop", "update", "delete", "insert", "order", "group", "having", "limit", "or", "and", legal text, recipes ("1 cup or 2 cups"), math ("1=1", "x' = y"), names with apostrophes, French/Italian elisions (`l'été`, `dell'arte`), Irish/Scottish names, possessives, quoted dialogue.
2. Add structured benign JSON/XML/form bodies (API payloads with SQL-ish keys like `order`, `group`, `select`, `limit`, `offset`, `where`), HTTP headers (`Accept-Language: en-US,en;q=0.9`, `User-Agent` strings with `;`, `--` in version strings), cookies and URLs (`/search?q=select+shoes`).
3. Add password-like strings: passphrases, strings with quotes and hashes (`P@ss'word#1`), symbols-heavy generated passwords, and sample passwords ending in `'--` as documented ambiguous cases with expected soft outcomes.
4. Implement the gate with expected classes: `no_match`, `soft_only(max_score)`; any hard match or block is a failure.
5. Provide a tool to add real-world false-positive reports to the corpus (scrubbed) with provenance.
6. Measure soft-score distributions to tune weights *without* touching hard families.
7. Report per-rule near-miss counts (benign entries with soft scores) so tuning is data-driven.
8. Document the policy and workflow.
**Deliverables.**
- extended corpora, gate, tools, reports
**Acceptance.**
- A1: at least 600 SQL-prose and 200 structured entries exist.
- A2: zero hard matches and zero blocks at Balanced paranoia 1 and 2 across all contexts.
- A3: ambiguous password cases behave per C4.6.
- A4: a deliberately over-broad rule (meta-test) fails the gate.
- A5: near-miss report is generated.
**Tests.**
- Gate and meta-tests.
**Notes.** The corpus is original material written for this project.
