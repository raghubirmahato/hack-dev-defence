### T-0170 · Pause, resume and emergency bypass semantics
`P08` `[API][UI][SEC]` · deps: T-0168 · size: M · owner: api
**Objective.** Define and implement safe ways to stop protecting a domain without breaking it.
**Steps.**
1. Define the modes: **pause protection** (pass-through: TLS, routing and headers stay; no inspection, jail or limits) time-boxed 15 min to 24 h with auto-resume; **DNS-only bypass** (records switched to grey in HDD's zone in `full_ns` mode, or user-guided steps in partial modes) for situations where HDD proxying must stop.
2. Implement the pause toggle with big warning, reason (optional), duration, and audit; publish snapshot with `safe_mode_until`/`paused` flag; the data plane honors it within 2 s.
3. Implement auto-resume jobs and notifications before and at expiry.
4. Implement the DNS-only bypass for `full_ns`: one-click converting proxied records to grey with a preview of resulting answers, an undo, and a warning about origin exposure; for partial modes provide exact instructions.
5. Implement the "emergency bypass instructions" page that works even if the app UI is unreachable (included in docs and in the email sent when enabling).
6. Implement permissions and step-up (`PERM-domain.pause`).
7. Implement metrics and attention items ("Protection paused until 14:30").
8. Test auto-resume with the clock and the data plane honoring (P11/P18).
**Deliverables.**
- services, UI, docs, tests
**Acceptance.**
- A1: paused domains auto-resume on schedule even if the owner is offline.
- A2: bypass preview shows exactly which records change and is reversible.
- A3: the pause banner appears on all domain pages while active.
- A4: audit events record who paused and why.
- A5: pause cannot exceed 24 h without re-confirmation.
**Tests.**
- Integration with the test clock; unit: duration validation; live checks in P18.
**Notes.** Pause differs from platform "safe mode" (global, P22).
