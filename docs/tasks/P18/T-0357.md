### T-0357 · HTTP/2 server hardening
`P18` `[DP][SEC]` · deps: T-0353, T-0354 · size: XL · owner: dp
**Objective.** Contain h2 resource-exhaustion attacks with enforceable limits.
**Steps.**
1. Configure the `h2` server with limits from Appendix E4: max concurrent streams 100, header list size 64 KiB, initial window sizes bounded, max frame size 16 KiB, HPACK dynamic table 4096 B and decoded header size cap, max pending accept reset streams, and connection-level flow-control limits.
2. Implement frame-rate limiters wrapping the transport/frame layer (own frame sniffer where `h2` lacks hooks): RST_STREAM at most 100 per 10 s per connection (Rapid Reset), then GOAWAY(ENHANCE_YOUR_CALM); SETTINGS at most 10 per 10 s; PING at most 10 per s; empty DATA frames at most 100 per 10 s; PRIORITY frames limited (ignored per RFC 9113 deprecation, with a counter); WINDOW_UPDATE flood limits; CONTINUATION header-block size cap and frame count cap (CONTINUATION flood).
3. Implement stream-level protections: cancel streams exceeding header size, reject streams that send pseudo-headers after regular headers, malformed pseudo-headers, duplicate pseudo-headers, uppercase header names, connection-specific headers (`connection`, `keep-alive`, `proxy-connection`, `transfer-encoding`, `upgrade`), `te` other than `trailers`.
4. Implement idle/keepalive timers, GOAWAY on graceful shutdown, and limits on total streams per connection lifetime (e.g., 10,000) forcing reconnection.
5. Disable h2c upgrade and prior-knowledge cleartext HTTP/2 unless explicitly enabled for internal listeners.
6. Implement metrics (frames by type, resets, GOAWAY reasons) and events (`proto` subtype `h2_abuse`) with ban policy for repeated abusers.
7. Build raw-socket attack generators in `hdd-acceptance` (`protocol-abuse` profile: 5 connections, 2,000 frames, 10 MiB caps): rapid reset, CONTINUATION flood, HPACK bomb, SETTINGS/PING/empty-frame floods, priority flood, window-update flood; cross-check with `h2spec` as an oracle in the builder.
8. Test that the process stays healthy (memory/CPU bounds) during attacks.
**Deliverables.**
- hardened h2 layer, generators, tests
**Acceptance.**
- A1: each attack class is contained: connection closed with GOAWAY or reset within the thresholds and the process remains healthy (memory growth under 5%, p99 for other clients within 2x).
- A2: `h2spec` conformance results are recorded (oracle) with documented deviations only for intentional hardening.
- A3: invalid pseudo-header and connection-specific header requests are rejected.
- A4: limits are configurable only within safe ranges (validated).
- A5: metrics count each abuse class.
**Tests.**
- Raw-socket attack suite (sandbox/builder only); `h2spec`; fuzz of frame parsing.
**Notes.** CVE-2023-44487 mitigation is verified here; CONTINUATION flood (2024) likewise.
