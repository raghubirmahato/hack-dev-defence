### T-0017 · Journey maps J-13 to J-24 (DNS, TLS, protection, appeals, lockdown)
`P01` `[DOC]` · deps: T-0016 · size: M · owner: ux
**Objective.** Map the operation and protection journeys, with particular attention to safety (self-lockout, collateral damage, rollbacks).
**Steps.**
1. Create maps for J-13 to J-24 with the table format of T-0016.
2. For each destructive or risky step add the safeguard (preview, confirmation, undo, time box) and the exact copy.
3. For J-16 and J-17 define the "monitor to block" decision support (what data convinces the user, what warning is honest).
4. For J-19 define the rule-builder error taxonomy (syntax, semantic, performance) and messages.
5. For J-22 and J-23 define the visitor experience end to end including the abuse resistance of the appeal flow.
6. For J-24 define the origin-lockdown steps per stack (ufw, iptables, nftables, cloud security groups, nginx/apache snippets) at the conceptual level and the verification probes.
7. Add the "what could go wrong" table per journey with the recovery action.
8. Record questions for the operator in the register where policy decisions are needed (for example default appeal retention).
**Deliverables.**
- `docs/UX/journeys/J-13.md` to `J-24.md`
**Acceptance.**
- A1: every risky step has a documented safeguard.
- A2: J-16 and J-17 include the decision-support content list.
- A3: J-22 describes at least five abuse cases against the appeal flow and their defenses.
- A4: J-24 lists verification probes that can be performed by HDD.
- A5: cross references to ATK IDs are present for protection steps.
**Tests.**
- `xtask ux check`.
**Notes.** Safeguards defined here become acceptance criteria in P11 to P17 tasks.
