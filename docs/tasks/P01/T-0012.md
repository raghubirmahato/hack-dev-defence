### T-0012 · Personas and jobs-to-be-done
`P01` `[DOC]` · deps: T-0011 · size: M · owner: ux
**Objective.** Convert B1 into reviewable persona documents with scenarios and accessibility needs.
**Steps.**
1. Copy B1.1 to B1.7 into `docs/UX/personas/*.md`, one file per persona, adding context-of-use details: device, connectivity, interruptions, vocabulary, what they check first each day.
2. For each persona write three scenarios in the "When ..., I want to ..., so I can ..." form with a measurable outcome.
3. Add an "anti-goals" section per persona (what will make them abandon the product).
4. Add accessibility needs and assistive technology assumptions per persona (screen reader, magnification, voice control, switch access, keyboard-only, color-vision deficiency, cognitive load).
5. Add the adversary persona with abuse stories as a security-UX input.
6. Cross-link personas to journeys (J-01 to J-34) and screens.
7. Ask the operator (in the register) which persona is primary for the pilot and record the answer.
8. Commit.
**Deliverables.**
- `docs/UX/personas/{maya,dan,sara,omar,vi,alex,adversary}.md`
- persona-journey matrix `docs/UX/persona-journey-matrix.md`
**Acceptance.**
- A1: each persona has at least three scenarios with measurable outcomes.
- A2: every journey J-01 to J-34 is linked to at least one persona.
- A3: accessibility needs are explicit and map to B11.11 criteria.
- A4: the adversary document lists at least ten abuse stories mapped to ATK IDs.
- A5: the operator's primary-persona answer is recorded or marked open.
**Tests.**
- Link check; matrix completeness check (script in xtask `ux check`).
**Notes.** Personas are fictional composites and must say so in the document header.
