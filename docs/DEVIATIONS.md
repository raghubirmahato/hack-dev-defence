Every loosened value, substituted technology or assumption is recorded here with reason, date and approver. The gate report lists new entries.
Seeded entries `DEV-V5-000` to `DEV-V5-007` are the ones the specification itself records (Appendix E9); `DEV-V5-004` and `DEV-V5-005` are conditional.
