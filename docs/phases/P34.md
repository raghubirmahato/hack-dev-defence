## Phase introduction (verbatim)

Requirement prefix `HRD`. Goal: close the three gaps that remain after the deterministic engine: (1) **unusual techniques** that no signature knows, with bounded and explainable machine-learning assistance (Sentinel, C20); (2) **bypass**: after a domain and its requirements are set, no request reaches the origin except through HDD, enforced structurally where possible and verified continuously everywhere else (Bypass Guard, C21, rule R-34); (3) **well-known vulnerabilities**: a knowledge base and pipeline that turn named CVEs and vulnerability classes into tested, signed, staged rules, plus the hardening measures of C22.5. Read C20, C21, C22 and rules R-33 and R-34 first.

Design rules for this phase:
- The deterministic engine stays authoritative. Learned components are advisory-first, bounded, explainable, signed, shadow-tested and never the only reason to ban (R-33).
- Nothing in this phase claims that attacks cannot succeed or that bypass is impossible. Claims cite measurements and verification dates (section 4).
- The analyst (a language model) is optional, off by default, never on the request path and never applies anything by itself.
- Where a task needs the runtime state, it uses the `KvStore`, `Bus` and `Limiter` traits (R-31).

**Execution order within P34 (the numbering is not the order).** T-0579 to T-0587 run after P13 and P14 (the engine and the SQLi shield) and P17 (behavior). T-0588 to T-0592 run after P21 (origin protection) and should be scheduled early in delivery slice DS3, because they decide whether a site may be called protected. T-0593, T-0597 and T-0598 run after P15. T-0594 and T-0595 run after P27; T-0596 runs after T-0595, T-0597 and T-0598. T-0599 and gate G34 close the phase.

Gate G34 passes when (each criterion PASS, or UNVERIFIED only with the enumerated reasons the operator has accepted in writing): AC-59 (Sentinel safety, robustness and shadow evidence), AC-60 (Bypass Guard: signed authentication, tunnel, firewall generators, continuous verification and the go-live gate), AC-61 (known-vulnerability coverage: VKB pipeline and ATK-301 to ATK-390) and AC-62 (additional hardening: parser differentials, inspection-window evasion, cache guard, deception, AI-endpoint protections) pass; no learned component can ban alone (tested); a site cannot enter `block` mode while its lockdown is not verified (tested); the held-out evaluation, including leave-one-family-out runs, is published with its limits; and the coverage map (E15) is regenerated with the new rows.

## Gate G34 pass condition (E5.4.1, normative)

> Gate G34 passes when: AC-59 (Sentinel safety, robustness and shadow evidence), AC-60 (Bypass Guard), AC-61 (known-vulnerability coverage and ATK-301 to ATK-390) and AC-62 (additional hardening) pass or end `UNVERIFIED` with enumerated reasons; no learned component can ban alone (tested); a site cannot enter `block` mode while its lockdown is not verified (tested); the held-out evaluation, including leave-one-family-out runs, is published with its limits; and the coverage map (E15) is regenerated with the new rows and no covered row without evidence.
