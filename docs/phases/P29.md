## Phase introduction (verbatim)

Requirement prefix `PRF`. Goal: prove the system is fast enough, survives faults, resists malformed input at scale, and ships from a trustworthy build pipeline: HTTP performance measurement (AC-24), soak and capacity, automated chaos, the consolidated fuzzing program, mutation and coverage gates, dependency and supply-chain controls (AC-33), unsafe-code policy enforcement, signed releases and self-update, and a structured security review of HDD itself.

Non-negotiables: performance numbers are measured and labeled by environment (lab, pilot VPS) and never extrapolated to internet scale; load runs against live infrastructure happen only in operator-announced windows on the pilot with the `load` profile; fuzzing never runs on the production VPS; supply-chain exceptions have owners and expiry; an unsigned or tampered artifact can never be installed; every security-relevant finding is tracked to closure or explicit risk acceptance.

Gate G29 passes when: AC-24 numbers meet the budgets (or documented deviations exist with tracked tasks); soak runs show no leaks; chaos scenarios keep invariants (no fail-open in `block` mode); fuzz smoke is clean and the nightly campaign has no open crash; mutation and coverage gates pass; AC-33 passes (signed update with rollback exercised); and the self-security review has no open high finding.
