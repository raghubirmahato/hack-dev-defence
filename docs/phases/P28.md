## Phase introduction (verbatim)

Requirement prefix `ACC`. Goal: complete the evidence machine. The acceptance runner reaches full capability, the safety envelope is proven by self-tests, the Vuln-Lab provides genuinely vulnerable applications in an isolated closed lab, and every remaining acceptance criterion is executed with honest PASS / FAIL / UNVERIFIED(reason) statuses. The centerpiece is the proof the product exists for: a vulnerable site, exploitable when accessed directly, is not exploitable through HDD, and the attacker's IP is banned (AC-38, AC-39).

Non-negotiables: the live suite touches only the profile's `test_hostname`, `sandbox_hostname` and the operator's own declared targets; Class B and body-bearing payloads go only to the sandbox or the Vuln-Lab; the inspect-first gate precedes any live enforcement attempt; the runner's IP is never in `admin_cidrs` and is protected by the never-ban list unless a test explicitly uses a second source; test bans use 60 s TTLs; external oracles (sqlmap, nikto, wapiti, nuclei, ZAP baseline, ffuf) run **only** against Vuln-Lab addresses; lab results are labeled `lab-proven` and never presented as live results; the Vuln-Lab never ships (release gate).

Gate G28 passes when: the runner executes the complete catalog AC-00 to AC-54 (AC-55 to AC-62 are registered and stay `UNVERIFIED(stage_not_reached)` until P33 and P34) with a rendered report in which every item is PASS, FAIL (none allowed at the gate) or UNVERIFIED with an enumerated reason; AC-38 and AC-39 pass in the lab including the oracle campaign figures; the safety self-tests (AC-00) pass; and no acceptance traffic left its allowlist (audit and packet-capture proof).
