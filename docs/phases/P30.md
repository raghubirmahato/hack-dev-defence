## Phase introduction (verbatim)

Requirement prefix `OPS`. Goal: make production operation boring: a final deployment architecture and installer, a complete `hddctl deploy`, backups with restore drills, upgrade and migration discipline, monitoring and alerting with runbooks, key rotation, environment management and a full disaster-recovery drill.

Non-negotiables: binaries are built on the builder and installed signed (never compiled on the VPS); every host-changing command has `--dry-run`, an exact undo and the timed auto-revert pattern where connectivity could be lost (section 2.5); backups are encrypted and restore-tested; secrets are generated and stored per policy (0600 files, systemd credentials, never in images or compose files); production changes follow the announced-window rules (section 2.7).

Gate G30 passes when: a clean VPS can be brought to a working production-shaped deployment by `hddctl deploy` alone; a total-loss disaster recovery drill meets the stated RTO/RPO; an upgrade and a forced rollback succeed; key rotations complete without downtime; monitoring, alert rules and runbooks cover every alert; and AC-54 passes.
