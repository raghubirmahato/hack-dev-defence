## PHASE P31: Documentation, cutover readiness and final report (T-0531 to T-0540) · milestone M12 · gate G31

Requirement prefix `FIN`. Goal: finish the product for real use: complete documentation, legal templates, a final pre-launch review, claims audit, the finalized cutover pack, the operator's STOP #2 package, the one-time production certificate issuance, the supervised cutover and stabilization, and the final handoff report. Actions affecting production delegation, certificates and public exposure happen only after the operator's written confirmation.

Non-negotiables: no registrar, DNS-delegation or production-certificate action is performed without the operator's explicit written go-ahead in chat for that specific action (section 4, R-04); cutover gates are evaluated item by item and any NO-GO item needs the operator's written risk acceptance for that item; the agent never presents the product as "unhackable" or "complete protection" (claims policy); everything that could not be verified is listed, not hidden.

**Execution order within P31** (the numbering is not the order): T-0531 to T-0536, then **T-0538** (production issuance), then **T-0537** (supervised cutover, which depends on it), then T-0539 and T-0540.

Gate G31 passes when: documentation is complete and tested; the legal templates and claims audit are done; the cutover pack passes its checklist and rehearsal; the operator confirmations are recorded; production issuance is completed or explicitly deferred; the cutover (if authorized) is completed and stabilized with monitor-first findings addressed, or explicitly deferred; and the final report with residual risks is delivered.
