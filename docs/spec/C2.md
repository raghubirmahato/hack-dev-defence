## C5. Attack coverage matrix (390 classes)

Every row is a requirement. `cargo xtask attack-matrix check` (T-0313) fails if a row lacks (a) at least one fixture file under `tests/fixtures/<group>/`, (b) at least one automated test that references the `ATK-nnn` ID, or (c) a mapped task. A row may be marked `limit` (known limitation) instead of detected, but then it must appear in `SECURITY.md` and the S-003 page with the reason.

**Action codes.** `BB` block and ban (hard signature, ban policy of C6 applies); `B` block without automatic ban (ban only through repetition rules); `S` soft score contribution; `C` challenge (proof of work); `L` log and alert only; `R` protocol-level rejection (400/413/414/421/431/501) with connection handling; `T` tarpit option; `P` posture finding (scanner/readiness, no runtime action); `Q` quota or limit enforced at the data plane. **PL** is the paranoia level at which the rule becomes active (1 default; 2 to 4 progressively stricter). **Ref** gives CWE and OWASP mapping (Top 10 2021 `A0x`, API Top 10 2023 `APIx`) to be rechecked against the current editions at build time.

Group acceptance: AG-01 AC-07, AC-38, AC-39; AG-02 to AG-07 AC-07, AC-09, AC-49; AG-08 AC-14; AG-09 AC-11, AC-35, AC-44; AG-10 AC-50; AG-11 AC-48; AG-12 AC-11, AC-14, AC-44; AG-13 AC-03, AC-28; AG-14 AC-26, AC-29; AG-15 AC-09, AC-31; AG-16 AC-10, AC-32; AG-17 AC-01, AC-35, AC-36, AC-42; AG-18 AC-09, AC-31, AC-61; AG-19 AC-14, AC-61, AC-62; AG-20 AC-61, AC-62; AG-21 AC-61, AC-62.
